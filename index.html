A quiet page before each prayer

Five to thirty minutes before a prayer, what’s on screen blurs and one page takes over: Asr in 15 minutes. Answer with I’ve prayed already, I’m going to pray now, or Remind me in 5 minutes.

It comes only while the app is open, never as a notification, and never during a call or while your screen is shared. Answered on one device, it clears on the rest.

The same pages on iPhone and Mac

Your list syncs through your own iCloud, so a task checked on one is checked on the other.

On iPhone

  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen: the prayer line, and seals you can tap to keep
  • Add a task with Siri, Shortcuts, Control Center or the Action button
  • Share a link from Safari straight into your Inbox

On Mac

  • The star in the menu bar, opening on today’s prayer times
  • Quick capture from any app, on keys you choose
  • A key for everything: ⌘N to write, ⌘F to find, ⌘[ and ⌘] between days

No account,

Questions

When does a day end?

At 4am, so anything you do before then counts for the day before. You can move it anywhere from midnight to 6am in Settings ▸ Day.

Where do my checked tasks go?

They stay on Today, ticked, until the day ends, then move to History. Swipe right to go back through every page you’ve kept.

What does +3 beside a task mean?

It’s been carried over for three days. After a week it turns red and offers to move to Some day, a tray at the foot of the page for what isn’t for now.

Can I use it without the prayer times?

Yes. The prayer line, the Hijri date and the Bismillah on a blank page each turn off in Settings, and the page colour can be set to day or night on its own.

My prayer times look wrong.

Check Settings ▸ Prayer: the location, the calculation method, the Asr rule and, far north or south, the high latitude rule. Adjust Times moves any prayer by up to 30 minutes to match your mosque.

Does it sync between my iPhone and Mac?

Yes, through your own iCloud. Sign both in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on for Rūznāma. Settings ▸ Rūznāma ▸ iCloud says when the pages last synced, or what’s in the way.

What is the voice model?

An optional 1.56 GB download in Settings ▸ Capture that lets you speak English and Arabic together. It downloads over Wi‑Fi and runs on your device.

Can I get my tasks out?

Settings ▸ Rūznāma ▸ Export the Book saves every page as one Markdown file, with its dates, sections and checkboxes.

Something isn’t working.

Write to support@ruznama.app and say which device and system version you’re on. More answers are on the support page.
