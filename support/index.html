A daily journal for your to-dos.

Each day gets one page. What you don't finish waits on tomorrow's, and what you do is kept in History.

Questions

When does a day end?

At 4am, so anything you do before then counts for the day before. Change it in Settings ▸ Day, anywhere from midnight to 6am.

Where did my checked tasks go?

They stay on Today until the day ends, then move to History. Swipe right to go back through past pages.

What does +3 beside a task mean?

It's been carried over for three days. After a week it turns red and offers to move to Some day.

My prayer times look wrong.

Check Settings ▸ Prayer: the location, the calculation method, the Asr rule and, far north or south, the high-latitude rule. Adjust Times moves any prayer by up to 30 minutes to match your mosque.

My pages aren't on my other iPhone.

Both need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on for Rūznāma. Settings ▸ Rūznāma ▸ iCloud says when the pages last synced, or what's in the way.

What is the voice model?

An optional 1.56 GB download (Settings ▸ Capture) that lets you speak English and Arabic together. It downloads over Wi-Fi and runs on your iPhone.

Can I get my tasks out?

Settings ▸ Rūznāma ▸ Export the Book saves every page as one Markdown file.
