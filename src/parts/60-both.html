The same pages on iPhone and Mac

Your list syncs through your own iCloud, so a task checked on one is checked on the other.

On iPhone

  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen: the prayer line, and seals you can tap to keep
  • Add a task with Siri, Shortcuts, Control Center or the Action button
  • Share a link from Safari straight into your Inbox

On Mac

  • The star in the menu bar, opening on today’s prayer times
  • Quick capture from any app, on keys you choose
  • A key for everything: ⌘N to write, ⌘F to find, ⌘[ and ⌘] between days

No account,
no tracking.

Just your iCloud. There’s no sign up, no ads, no analytics and no server of mine: your pages stay on your devices and sync through your private iCloud.

Prayer times, your voice and the sorting are all worked out on the device. Turn off iCloud and it all still works.
