Questions

How do I get it?

It’s in beta for now, through Apple’s TestFlight. Open the beta link on your iPhone, install TestFlight when it asks, then tap Accept and Install. On a Mac, download it and drag it to Applications, or install it with Homebrew: brew install --cask omareletr/tap/ruznama. It needs macOS 26.

What does it cost?

Nothing while it’s in beta. It will probably cost something later, likely as a subscription. Everyone who joins before then is a founding member and gets a lower price for good.

When does a day end?

At 4am, so anything you tick before then counts for the day before. You can move it anywhere between midnight and 6am in Settings ▸ Day.

Where do my checked tasks go?

They stay on Today, ticked, until the day ends, then move to History. Swipe right to go back through every page you’ve kept.

What does +3 beside a task mean?

It’s been carried over for three days. After a week it turns red and offers to move to Some day, a tray at the foot of the page for things that aren’t for now.

Can I use it without the prayer times?

Yes. The prayer line, the Hijri date and the Bismillah on a blank page each turn off in Settings, and the page colour can be set to day or night on its own.

My prayer times look wrong.

Check Settings ▸ Prayer: the location, the calculation method, the Asr rule and, far north or south, the high latitude rule. Adjust Times moves any prayer by up to 30 minutes to match your mosque.

Does it sync between my iPhone and Mac?

Yes, through your own iCloud. Sign both in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on for Rūznāma. Settings ▸ Rūznāma ▸ iCloud says when the pages last synced, or what’s in the way.

Can I write in Arabic?

Yes. A task that starts in Arabic is set right to left, and you can speak Arabic to it too. The app’s own menus and labels are in English for now.

Is there an Android or Windows version?

No. Rūznāma is only for iPhone and Mac.

Can I get my tasks out?

Settings ▸ Rūznāma ▸ Export the Book saves every page as one Markdown file, with its dates, sections and checkboxes.

Something isn’t working.

Write to support@ruznama.app and say which device and system version you’re on. More answers are on the support page.
